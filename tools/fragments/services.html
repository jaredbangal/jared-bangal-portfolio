03

Portfolio Sites

Focused sites for showing work: photographers, designers, makers, studios. The design problem here is different from a business site — the work has to be the loudest thing on the page, and adding a new project later has to take minutes rather than a rebuild. So the layout is built to accept whatever you make next.

  • Layouts that hold up whether a project is one image or thirty
  • Image handling that stays fast — modern formats, sized properly, lazy-loaded
  • Adding a project is copying one block and changing the contents
  • Case-study pages if the thinking matters as much as the picture
from $1,200 Priced as a full site, since a portfolio is rarely one page.

How a project runs

Two to four weeks for a typical site. You see the design before anything gets built, and nothing moves forward without your sign-off.

  • Week 1 — Discover. A short call about your business, your audience, and what the site needs to do.
  • Week 1–2 — Design. Layout and visual direction, shared for feedback before anything is built.
  • Week 2–3 — Build. The site gets built, tested and refined with you along the way.
  • Week 4 — Launch. It goes live, with handoff notes and optional ongoing care.

You work with me directly the whole way through — there is no account manager and no handoff between teams.
