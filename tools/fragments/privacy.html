Privacy

What this site knows about you.

Very little, and this page says exactly what. No analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no advertising.

Last updated 15 August 2026

Status, as of the date above: the contact and newsletter forms on this site are not yet connected to anything. Submitting one does not transmit your details anywhere and does not reach me. Until that changes, email is the working route. This page will be updated when the forms go live.

Who is responsible

This site is run by Jared Bangal, a freelance web designer. Questions about anything on this page, including requests to see or delete what I hold, go to hello@jaredbangal.com.

What is collected

Forms

The contact form asks for your name, your email address, and an optional note about your project and budget. The newsletter form asks only for an email address. Nothing is required beyond that, and there are no hidden fields.

When these forms are connected, submissions will reach me by email and I will keep them as long as they are useful — a live enquiry until the project ends, an enquiry that goes nowhere for about a year. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Server logs

This site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs of requests: IP address, the page requested, the time, and the browser's user-agent string. That is normal infrastructure logging, it is not tied to you by name, and I do not read it routinely.

Fonts and scripts loaded from elsewhere

Two things on this site come from other companies' servers, which means your browser contacts them and they can see your IP address:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) serves the typeface.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) serves the 3D library that draws the particle field in the background.

Neither is under my control and both have their own privacy policies. Blocking them will make the site look plainer but it will still work.

What is not collected

  • No cookies. This site sets none at all.
  • No analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Meta pixel, nothing counting visits.
  • No local storage. Nothing is written to your browser between visits.
  • No profiling, no advertising, no automated decisions.

Who else sees it

Nobody buys, rents or receives your details for marketing. The only third parties involved are the ones that make the site work: Vercel, which hosts it, and the font and script providers named above. If a form submission arrives by email, my email provider handles it the way it handles any other message.

I would disclose information if the law required it. That has never happened.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — the UK, the EU, California and a growing number of other places — you have the right to ask what personal data I hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, and to object to how it is used.

The mechanism is the same for all of them: email hello@jaredbangal.com and say what you want. There is no form to fill in and no charge. Given how little is held, most requests are answered the same week.

Newsletter emails carry an unsubscribe link. Using it removes your address rather than flagging it.

Children

This site is aimed at business owners and is not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes — a new third party, a new kind of collection — will be described here rather than quietly absorbed.
