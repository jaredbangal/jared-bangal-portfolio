Products

Things you can buy without hiring me.

Not everyone needs a designer. Some people need a good starting point and a bit of guidance. That is what this will be.

Nothing is on sale yet. This page exists so you can see where it is going and put your name down. There is no checkout, no waiting-list fee, and no product being sold in advance of existing.

What this is for

A custom site is the right answer when a business has something specific to say and no time to build it. It is the wrong answer when someone has fifty dollars, a weekend, and the willingness to do it themselves — and plenty of good businesses start exactly there.

So the plan is a small set of digital products that hand over the useful part of the process without the invoice: the structure, the type and colour decisions, and the reasons behind them. Made by someone who builds these for a living, rather than assembled to fill a store.

What I am working towards

Deliberately vague, because announcing a product before it exists is how you end up shipping something worse to keep a promise. Broadly, the shape of it:

  • Starting points that are actually finished — properly responsive, properly accessible, and readable on a phone, rather than a desktop layout that collapses.
  • The decisions written down. Why this measure, why this contrast, why this order of sections — the part a template usually leaves you to guess.
  • Small enough to be worth it. If something needs a week of your time to use, it is not a product, it is homework.

Have something in mind?

The list of what gets built first is short and not fixed. If there is a thing you wish existed — a template for a specific trade, a guide to one part of it, a set of components — say so. Nobody has to be a customer to have an opinion about this.
