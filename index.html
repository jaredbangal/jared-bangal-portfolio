What I do

I build websites for small businesses that need to look like they mean it.

Most of the people I work with are one or two of them, doing good work, with no time to fight a page builder. I take the whole thing off your hands — the design, how the page is put together, the build, and the handoff — so what you end up with is a site you can actually run.

  • Small businesses and startups

    Studios, shops, trades, and one-person practices. Work that deserves better than a template.

  • You talk to the person building it

    No account manager, no handoff between teams. The same person scopes it, designs it, and ships it.

  • Built to hand over

    You own the site and everything in it, with notes on how to keep it going once I'm gone.
