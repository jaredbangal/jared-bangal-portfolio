How we roast

Small Twelve kilos at a time

Small enough to profile each lot on its own terms, and to stop a roast that is not going where we want it.

Light Stopped before second crack

We roast to taste the farm rather than the roaster. That means brighter cups than a supermarket bag, and it is deliberate.

Fresh Bagged the same morning

Roasted Monday and Thursday, shipped that afternoon. Rest it three days before you brew — it is better on day five than day one.
