Full service

What you actually get

  • [✓]Frame stripped and degreased, bearings checked
  • [✓]Drivetrain cleaned or replaced, chain wear measured
  • [✓]Gears indexed, cables and outers replaced
  • [✓]Brakes bled or pads replaced, rotors trued
  • [✓]Both wheels trued and tension balanced
  • [✓]Every bolt torqued to spec and recorded
  • [✓]Test ride before you collect it

You get the torque sheet with the bike. It lists what was adjusted, what was replaced, and what we think will need doing in the next few months — so the next shop, or you, is not guessing.

Anything we find that is not on the job sheet gets a phone call, not a surprise on the invoice. If you would rather we just did it, say so when you drop it off and we will work to a ceiling you set.

Before you drop in

Worth knowing

We work on anything

Road, gravel, mountain, cargo, kids' bikes and the twenty-year-old hybrid in your shed. No brand snobbery and no minimum spend.

E-bikes, mostly

We service Bosch, Shimano and Fazua systems. Proprietary batteries on some direct-to-consumer brands we cannot legally open — call first.

Drop-off, not appointment

Bring it any time we are open. Booking reserves bench time, which is what makes the three-to-five day turnaround hold.

Free adjustment after

Cables stretch and gears drift in the first fortnight. Bring it back within thirty days and we will re-index it for nothing.
