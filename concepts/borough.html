The shop

Four chairs, a tiled floor, and a radio that stays on one station. Borough opened in 2019 in a unit that had been a launderette for thirty years, and we kept the tiling because it was better than anything we'd have put in.

It's a barber shop, not a salon. That means no colour, no treatments, and nobody trying to sell you product on the way out. It also means you can turn up on a Tuesday and be out in forty minutes.

  • 01Cash and card both fine. No minimum on card.
  • 02Bring a photo if you have one. It helps more than a description.
  • 03Kids are welcome. Weekdays before four are quietest.
  • 04Running more than fifteen minutes late means we may need to rebook you.
  • 05If the cut isn't right, come back within a week and we'll fix it free.

Before you come in

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, whenever a chair is open. Same-day slots go live at eight each morning, and that is usually the better bet — weekends fill by Thursday.

How do I cancel?

Same link as the booking, up to four hours before. After that we'd rather you called the shop than left the chair empty.

Can I ask for a specific barber?

You can, and there's no extra charge. It may mean waiting a few days longer — Sal in particular books out.

Do you do beards only?

Yes, twenty-five minutes and thirty dollars. Worth booking rather than walking in, since it's a short slot to find.
