Pre-order,
and skip the queue

1 Order by Thursday

Tell us what you want and which day you are collecting. Thursday at six is the cut-off, because the flour is weighed on Friday.

2 We bake it that morning

Pre-orders come out of the same ovens at the same time as everything else. Nothing is baked the day before and held.

3 Collect before noon

Your name is on the shelf behind the till. After one, anything uncollected goes out to the counter.

Allergens: everything is baked in a kitchen that handles wheat, dairy, egg, nuts and sesame. Ask us and we will tell you exactly what is in any loaf.
