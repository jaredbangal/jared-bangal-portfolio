The studio

Botanica is a two-person studio on Clarkson Street. We buy at the market four mornings a week and grow the rest on a leased half-acre upstate — which is why the list is short in February and unreasonable in June.

We do not carry imported stems out of season, and we would rather talk you into something at its peak than sell you a photograph of a peony in November.

Everything on the bench was alive last week and will not be alive next month. That is not a limitation of the work. It is the work. Elena Vasquez, founder

Weddings and installations
